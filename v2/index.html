Arunachal Dam Watch II

We put together the numbers from two government websites to see how many dams are planned, how much forest they need, and whether the two websites tell the same story.

Where the numbers come from. All power figures come from DHPD, the state's hydropower projects website. All forest figures come from Parivesh, the national website for forest permissions. We never mix the two.

    Chapter 2 · The forest

    Dams flood land and need roads, tunnels and workers' camps. To use forest land for that, a company has to ask the government for permission. This is called a forest clearance. The requests are filed on a website called Parivesh.

    Forest area is counted in hectares (ha). A football pitch is about 0.7 ha, so the pitch comparison is only a rough guide. Each project is counted once, even if it applied more than once.

    "Fully approved" means the government has given final permission. It does not mean the trees have been cut. This data does not say how much forest has actually been cleared on the ground.

    Chapter 7 · Read this before you quote a number

    What this page can and cannot tell you

    • Approved is not the same as cleared on the ground. The data says permission was given. It does not say how many trees were cut.
    • Parivesh is missing recent requests. Our Parivesh file only covers requests filed before 28 June 2022. A dam with no record may simply have applied later.
    • We matched the lists by dam name. Names are spelled differently on the two sites, so a few matches were made by hand. They are listed on the last tab.
    • The numbers are a floor, not the full picture. They cover only what the two websites show.
